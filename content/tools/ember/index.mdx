## What it is

Ember tracks small daily habits and shows them as a slowly brightening fire. Miss a day
and the fire dims, but it never goes out completely.

## Why it exists

I wanted a habit tracker that rewards consistency without punishing a missed day.
Most trackers reset a streak to zero, and that is usually the moment people stop.

## Features

<FeatureGrid>
  <Feature title="Daily check-in" icon="CircleCheck">
    One tap per habit. No forms, no notes unless you want them.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Soft streaks" icon="Flame">
    A missed day dims the fire instead of resetting it.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Weekly glow" icon="CalendarDays">
    A Sunday summary of which habits stayed warm.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Private by default" icon="Lock">
    Your data stays in your own database. No social feed.
  </Feature>
</FeatureGrid>

## How it is built

| Decision | Choice | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting | Vercel | Same platform as the rest of the suite |
| Database | Postgres | Habits and check-ins are plain relational data |
| Auth | Magic link | No passwords to manage for a personal tool |
